\author{Alexander Czernik}
\address{Université Jean Monnet Saint-Étienne}
\email{alexander.czernik@univ-st-etienne.fr}
\date{}
\subjclass[2020]{60H15, 35L71, 37L40}
\keywords{stochastic damped wave equation, invariant measure,

\begin{abstract}
We study invariant measures for a Wick-renormalized mean-field stochastic damped wave equation on the two-dimensional torus and for the corresponding stochastic heat equation. With Wick ordering relative to the massive Gaussian free field with covariance \((1-\Delta)^{-1}\), we prove uniqueness among invariant laws whose renormalized second moment belongs to \(H^{-\sigma}(\mathbb T^2)\), where \(0<\sigma<\tfrac12\). Stationarity reduces the mean-field equation to a linear equation with a deterministic distribution-valued potential. We show that self-consistency forces this deterministic potential to vanish. The damped wave equation therefore has invariant law \(\mathcal N(0,(1-\Delta)^{-1})\otimes\mathcal N(0,\varepsilon^{-2}I)\), with covariance understood distributionally, while the heat equation has the Gaussian free field as its invariant law. This resolves self-consistency for spatially varying distribution-valued potentials and establishes uniqueness in the specified class.
As the inertia parameter tends to zero, the position marginal remains equal to the invariant law of the corresponding stochastic heat equation, whereas the velocity marginal assigns vanishing probability to every bounded subset of \(H^{-1-\sigma}(\mathbb T^2)\), establishing Smoluchowksi-Kramers type behavior. 
\end{abstract}
